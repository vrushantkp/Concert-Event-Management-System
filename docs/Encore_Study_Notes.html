01 · Explain

Project in 30 seconds

“Encore is a concert ticketing demo. A customer finds a show, chooses seats or asks for adjacent-seat suggestions, places a five-minute hold, and confirms to receive a QR ticket. PostgreSQL manages event inventory and bookings with transactions and row locks. MongoDB stores customer preferences. The organizer manages events, and the administrator can demonstrate live database operations.”

Customer experience

Browse and filter shows, see the schedule and ticket prices, jump to the seat map with Book tickets, and manage bookings and preferences in My Encore.

Example show

Campus After Hours is a fictional PES University Bengaluru listing. It has 90 demo seat records across Main Floor, Gallery, and Balcony, with prices seeded from ₹499. Confirming a booking does not charge money.

03 · Draw

Data model and key constraints

For a whiteboard answer, draw these relationships. The full diagram appears in the DBMS Explorer and at public/erd.png.

Tables or collectionWhat to say in the viva
venue → section → seatA venue has sections. A section has numbered seats in rows. The section supplies the distance used for recommendations.
event, artist, categoryAn event links its artist, category, venue, and organizer. Store a venue once and reuse it across events. Categories can link to a parent category.
event_seat and ticket_category(event_id, seat_id) uniquely identifies one seat for one performance. The same physical seat can be sold for a different event. Composite foreign keys enforce a matching venue and event.
booking → booking_seatA booking gets a UUID, customer, event, status, and expiry. Child rows store the selected seat IDs and capture their prices at hold time.
event_seat.holder and audit_logThe holder points to the current booking for that event seat. The audit table keeps a timestamped history of booking status changes, including the original hold.
app_user, session, event_activityAccounts and their roles live in PostgreSQL. Session rows expire. Saved shows and recent views are attached to user and event IDs.
MongoDB preferencesThe unique userId index limits each PostgreSQL user ID to one preference document. The app connects the two records by user ID.

Indexes to remember. Published events by start time help discovery. Booking rows are indexed by customer and date for history. A partial expiry index covers held bookings. A unique MongoDB index speeds up lookup by user ID and prevents duplicate profiles.

Current price and booked price. ticket_category.price is the current selling price. booking_seat.price is the historical snapshot. Changing the first leaves the old booking total intact.

04 · Trace

A booking from click to database

  1. Open a show and click Book tickets. The show page jumps to the seat map. GET /api/inventory?eventId=… refreshes inventory about every eight seconds. Selecting a seat highlights it locally.
  2. Hold 1–8 seats. The browser sends IDs to POST /api/reserve. The server verifies the customer session and opens a transaction. reserve_seats locks event inventory rows in ascending seat ID order and checks availability again.
  3. Save the hold atomically. PostgreSQL creates a held booking with a five-minute expiry, captures line prices, and points the event seats at the new booking. If a requested seat fails the check, the transaction rolls back.
  4. Confirm or cancel. POST /api/confirm checks the customer's ownership, hold expiry, and seat ownership before moving the booking to confirmed. Cancellation changes status and clears the holder for the booking's seats while retaining its history.
  5. Show the evidence. The audit trigger writes the old and new status. The confirmation page creates a QR link to /ticket/[reference]. The verification route checks the stored booking status and whether the event is published.
HeldReserved until the deadline.
ConfirmedSeat is booked and a QR ticket is available.
CancelledHistory stays, seat becomes available.
ExpiredThe hold can no longer be confirmed.

Two customers, one seat

A locks the row and commits a hold. B waits for that lock, then rechecks the row and sees A's hold. B's request fails. The lock and the server-side check are what prevent a double booking.

When time runs out

An expired hold is reclaimable immediately. The Explorer's Expire holds procedure records expired on overdue rows, which also produces an audit entry. A screen timer is visual feedback. The database timestamp decides eligibility.

How seat suggestions work

lib/recommend.ts groups currently available seats by section and row, sorts by seat number, and considers windows of the requested size. It rejects gaps in numbering, groups exceeding the total budget, and groups without an accessible seat when one is requested. The section preference earns a bonus. It does not exclude other sections.

Scoring rule (the highest three candidates are returned) 
score = 40
      + 25 × (1 − total cost / max(budget, 1))
      + 15 if the section matches or no section is preferred
      + 20 / (1 + |distance − preferred distance| / 8)

Rounded to the nearest integer. Ties favor lower total cost.

Worked example. Two adjacent Gallery seats at ₹499 each cost ₹998. With a ₹1,200 budget, Gallery selected, and a preferred distance of 14 m (the Gallery distance), the group scores approximately 79/100. Two ₹999 Main Floor seats are excluded by that budget.

This is a rules-based ranking. The event-page Recommended shows section is separate and ranks upcoming events by matching artist, genre, then city. My Encore also uses saved preferences to find matching events.

05 · Defend

Login, roles, and permissions

Login verifies a salted scrypt hash. The browser stores an opaque token in an HTTP-only cookie. PostgreSQL stores a digest of the token and the session expires after 12 hours. Each protected request resolves the user and their current role on the server.

RoleWhat they can demonstrate
GuestBrowse published events, inspect availability, and open the limited ticket verification page.
CustomerReserve seats, see their own bookings, save shows, and update their own preferences.
OrganizerManage their own event listings and see their event inventory, confirmed booking value, and occupancy.
AdministratorManage event and venue data, user access, and run approved SQL and MongoDB examples in DBMS Explorer.

Explain the three checks. The page protects staff routes. The API checks roles before handling requests. PostgreSQL GRANT rules limit permitted operations, while row-level security (RLS) limits which rows a role can see or edit. The server uses SET LOCAL ROLE and a transaction-local user ID, so the connection pool can reuse the connection safely.

07 · Prepare

Run the local demo

On the prepared laptop

Open http://127.0.0.1:3000 when the app is running. If it has stopped, open PowerShell in encore and use the commands below. Keep PostgreSQL and MongoDB running.

On a teammate's laptop

Use the current source folder, Node.js 22 or newer, and local PostgreSQL and MongoDB. The project includes Compose for a fresh setup. Read README.md before entering credentials.

Prepared build and demo password (PowerShell) 
npm start -- --hostname 127.0.0.1
# In a second terminal in the same folder
Select-String -Path .env.local -Pattern '^DEMO_PASSWORD='

Use the password configured during seed setup with customer@encore.test, organizer@encore.test, or admin@encore.test. Keep the password out of the presentation and use the exact origin in APP_ORIGIN. Separate browser profiles can keep customer and admin sessions open at the same time.

Fresh setup with Docker Compose (PowerShell) 
Copy-Item .env.example .env.local
# Fill in the database settings and initial demo password in .env.local
npm ci
docker compose --env-file .env.local up -d
npm run db:setup
npm run build -- --webpack
npm start -- --hostname 127.0.0.1

Copy the env template only on a fresh setup. The migration account applies SQL and seed data. The app uses the separate runtime account. Database URLs, passwords, ports, and the app origin belong in the local env file. Use the initially seeded demo password if the database already exists.

Before presenting. Open the catalogue and sign in as customer and admin. If a rebuild is needed, stop the old app server before rebuilding and restarting it. Refresh with Ctrl+F5 if a browser still shows the previous styles.

Quick database proof

Use the Explorer when possible. If the examiner wants direct SQL, replace BOOKING_UUID with the reference from the ticket and run these read-only statements against the demo database.

SELECT b.id, b.status, b.expires_at, s.seat_id, s.price
FROM encore.booking b
JOIN encore.booking_seat s ON s.booking_id = b.id
WHERE b.id = 'BOOKING_UUID'::uuid;

SELECT old_status, new_status, happened_at
FROM encore.audit_log
WHERE booking_id = 'BOOKING_UUID'::uuid
ORDER BY id;

To inspect a preference directly in mongosh, get the customer ID from PostgreSQL first. In the MongoDB database selected by MONGODB_DB, run db.preferences.findOne({ userId: CUSTOMER_ID }) with that numeric value.

09 · Practice

Viva flashcards

Say your answer out loud before revealing the notes.

Why does the project need two databases?

PostgreSQL enforces inventory relationships and handles atomic booking transactions. MongoDB keeps a customer's preference arrays and optional choices together in one document. A preference update can happen independently from a booking.

How do we prevent two customers from booking the same seat?

There is one inventory row for the pair of event and seat IDs. The procedure locks that row, checks availability again, and writes the new holder in the same transaction. The second request waits, rechecks, and fails if the seat is held or booked.

What is the difference between a hold and a confirmed booking?

A hold reserves seats temporarily and carries a deadline. Confirmation checks ownership and expiry and produces a verifiable ticket. An expired hold becomes reclaimable.

What happens if one seat in a group is unavailable?

The procedure checks the whole group after locking it. If the available count differs from the number requested, it rejects the request. The transaction rolls back so the customer does not get part of the group.

Why keep the price on a booking line?

It records the price at hold time. If staff change the current category price later, the old ticket total remains correct.

What is row-level security?

A PostgreSQL policy that filters access to individual rows. In Encore, customers read their own bookings and organizers manage their own events. Grants determine which operations a role has permission to attempt.

How do a function, procedure, and trigger differ here?

We use a function such as booking_total in a query. We explicitly CALL a procedure such as confirm_booking. The audit trigger runs automatically when a booking is inserted or its status changes.

Why does the confirmed-booking report use a LEFT JOIN?

It keeps every event in the result, including events with no matching booking count. COALESCE turns the missing count into zero.

What is recursive about the category query?

It begins with root categories, joins to their children, then repeats on each new layer. Its path tracks visited IDs to prevent a cycle within a traversal.

How do the recommendations use accessibility?

If the customer requests accessibility, the group must contain at least one seat marked accessible. Contiguity and budget remain required, and the remaining groups are ranked with the scoring rule.

What does the QR code verify?

It opens a link containing a booking UUID. The server checks that the booking is confirmed and its show is published. The public page displays the show, time, and ticket status without customer details.

What belongs outside this local demo?

A real deployment would need payment processing if it charges customers, production infrastructure, monitoring, and stronger operational controls. The current show listings and payment-free bookings are for demonstration.
